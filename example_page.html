Example Analysis: Caesar, Catullus & Vergil

This page is designed to help you translate Latin sentences. The primary goal is not to identify individual forms, but to understand the function of words or word groups within the structure of the entire sentence. Adjectives and genitive attributes are therefore grouped with the words they modify. You can click on a word or group of words—or navigate to them using the Tab and Enter keys—to select their syntactical function.

Once you have correctly analyzed every word group in a sentence, a textbox will appear into which you can write your translation.

Caesar, De Bello Gallico 1.1

Gallia est omnis divisa in partes tres,
?quarum unam incolunt Belgae, aliam Aquitani, tertiam
?qui ipsorum lingua Celtae, nostra Galli appellantur.
Hi omnes lingua, institutis, legibus inter se differunt.
Gallos ab Aquitanis Garumna flumen, a Belgis Matrona et Sequana dividit.
