Latin Syntax Studio

Step 1: Setup Instruction

This page allows you to create your own sentences for syntactical analysis. The idea is that you give your students some support with the analysis and translation, e.g. by indicating which groups of words belong together, and that your students understand the syntax of a sentence before they start translating.

On this page, you will be asked to provide a title, optional notes for the students, a picture for the student page (optional) and a text or a few sentences. Then, click 'Copy AI Prompt' and paste the prompt into a large language model such as ChatGPT or Gemini. Finally, paste the JSON code the AI gives you into the box and click 'Import' or 'Start manual creation'.

Step 2: Editing Instruction

Revise the syntactic analysis. Adjacent words with same labels merge automatically, unless you use 'Manual Split' and click the first word to keep them separate. In 'Manual Split' mode, you can also click on multi-word chunks to separate them. To create larger syntactic groups, click 'Draw Bracket', click the start and end word (or simply Shift-Click two words), then type to select a label. You can click on a group's label to rename it. You can adjust the boundaries of existing groups by clicking the ◀ and ▶ arrows on their labels, or by Dragging and Dropping the arrows directly onto a word to quickly resize them. Double-click any word to correct typos, add helpful hints (💡, supports **bold** and *italics*), or delete it entirely. You can also add hints to groups when drawing a bracket. Use 'Toggle Break (J)' to manually force or suppress a sentence break. Use 'Undo' to revert your last action. Shortcuts: Press the underlined letter to select a label (and instantly apply it to a hovered word), 'X' for Split, 'K' for Bracket, 'J' to Toggle Break, Ctrl+Z to Undo. At the end, hit 'Export Student Page'.
